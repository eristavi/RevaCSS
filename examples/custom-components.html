Extension notes

Load custom-components.css after RevaCSS. Keep the component's card class to inherit material styling. Use unique heading IDs for every instance and replace these demonstration links with real application destinations.

Change html's data-theme to light, dark or auto. Change data-palette to a documented palette, or load your exported custom palette after the framework. Adding components and saving preferences belong to your application.

This is an HTML/CSS reference example. Browser, device and accessibility acceptance checks for this addition are deferred; a successful build does not establish conformance.
